What is {{ APP_NAME }}?

{{ APP_NAME }} is a chronic kidney disease (CKD) risk-screening tool built as an academic capstone project. From the information you provide, a trained machine-learning model estimates a risk level and shows which of your answers most affected that estimate.

Is a result a diagnosis?

No. A result — low or high — is a screening estimate, not a diagnosis. It does not confirm or rule out kidney disease, prescribe treatment, or replace a doctor. Always confirm anything that matters with a qualified healthcare professional and proper laboratory testing.

What is the difference between Preliminary and Enhanced Screening?

Preliminary Screening uses questionnaire and health-history answers only — no lab tests needed. Enhanced Screening adds laboratory values (for example creatinine and eGFR), so it is more precise but needs a recent lab report. You can start with the preliminary screening and do the enhanced one later.

Who can use {{ APP_NAME }}, and what are the account types?

You must be at least 18 to create an account. There are two sign-up roles: a General User, who screens themselves and keeps a private history, and a Healthcare Professional, who can review assessments that people choose to share with them.

Who can see my screening results?

Your screenings are private to your account. A healthcare professional can see them only if and when you choose to share them, and you can end that access at any time. Administrators manage accounts and the service but do not see a named person's results — only totals and de-identified data.

How is my information kept safe?

Passwords are stored only as a salted, slow hash — never in plain text — and the site is served over HTTPS. Your answers may be used to improve the model only with your name, contact details, gender and exact location removed. No online system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

What does the "verified" badge on a professional mean?

It means an administrator recorded a self-reported professional licence number. It is informational only and is not a guarantee of anyone's credentials or clinical competence.

Can I deactivate my account?

Yes. On Your account, use Deactivate account. Your data is kept, but you cannot sign in until an administrator switches the account back on. You will be asked for your password to confirm.

Can I permanently delete my account?

Yes. On Your account, use Delete account. This permanently removes your account and your own data — screenings, uploads, history and sharing — and cannot be undone. You will be asked for your password and to confirm before anything is deleted.

How accurate is the screening?

The models produce estimates, not certainties, and can be wrong in either direction. Measured performance for each model is published on the administrator's Model page. Unusual answers, or values outside the range the model was trained on, make an estimate less reliable.

I forgot my password — what do I do?

Contact the administrator for this deployment. They can issue a temporary password, which you will be asked to change the next time you sign in.
