1. Accepting these terms

By creating an account or using {{ APP_NAME }}, you agree to these Terms & Conditions. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

2. What {{ APP_NAME }} is

{{ APP_NAME }} is a kidney-disease (CKD) risk-screening and decision-support tool built as an academic capstone project. It estimates a risk level from the information you provide using a trained machine- learning model and shows which of your answers most affected that estimate. It is a screening aid only. It does not diagnose any condition, prescribe treatment, or replace professional medical evaluation or clinical judgment. A result — low or high — is never a statement that you do or do not have kidney disease.

3. Who may use it

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. By registering you confirm that the information you give is true and that any patient information a healthcare professional enters is handled with the patient's knowledge and consent.

5. Healthcare professional accounts

Professional accounts may view a person's assessments only after that person shares them, and may add clinical notes and follow-ups. Professionals are responsible for their own licensure and for their own clinical decisions. {{ APP_NAME }} does not provide medical advice through professionals using it.

6. Acceptable use

  • Use {{ APP_NAME }} only for its intended screening purpose and only for yourself or for people whose information you are authorised to handle.
  • Do not upload another person's lab report or health information without their consent.
  • Do not attempt to break, overload, probe, or gain unauthorised access to the service or other users' data.
  • Do not misrepresent who you are or your professional status.

7. Your information and privacy

  • Your results. Your screenings and results are private to your account and visible to a healthcare professional only if and when you choose to share them. You can end that access at any time.
  • Improving the model. Your screening answers may be used to improve {{ APP_NAME }}'s model with your name, contact details, gender and exact location removed.
  • Location. If you say where you live, it is shown to health officials only as totals per place, never tied to your name.
  • Administrators. Administrators manage accounts and the service. They do not see a named person's screening answers or results — only totals and de-identified data.
  • Security. Passwords are stored only as a salted, slow hash; the connection is served over HTTPS. No online system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

{{ APP_NAME }}'s models were trained on datasets for this project and produce estimates, not certainties. A result can be wrong in either direction. Values outside the range the model was trained on, or unusual combinations of answers, make an estimate less reliable. Always confirm anything that matters with a qualified healthcare professional and appropriate laboratory testing.
