{{ icon_chip('shield-check', 'lavender') }}The privacy rule

An administrator manages accounts, the re-assessment rule and the models. No administrator page shows a named person's screening answers, risk probabilities, or results. Health figures are totals only, each person counted once without a name, and small groups are hidden. The one row-level exception is Research data: de-identified, predictors only (no name, contact details, dates, gender, location or result), and every download is logged. Treat those files as health data.

{{ icon_chip('book-open', 'lavender') }}The sections

Overview
How many people use Nephron, how many were screened, how many are in the higher-risk group and in which age groups, professionals awaiting verification, and whether each model is ready.
Accounts
Search every account. Switch off stops a person signing in without deleting anything, and ends their open sessions at once. Reset password gives a temporary password, shown to you only once; the person must choose their own at their next sign-in.
Professional verification
Compare each stated licence number with the PRC's own records, outside Nephron, then mark it verified or not verified. Patients see the result when they search for a professional.
Re-assessment rule
How long a General User waits between screenings (3 months by default; 1 to 12 months, or none). A healthcare professional the person shares with can open one early screening, and so can you when the person asks: approve or decline their request here. The next screening uses it up, and the waiting period applies again from that day.
Model performance
Each model's measured performance (accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, precision, F1, ROC-AUC and its curve, the confusion matrix), which inputs weigh most in it, and its model card. The figures come from python model_report.py run on labelled test data - nothing is typed in.
Activity log
Sign-ins, failed sign-ins, new accounts, password changes, and every administrator action - who did it, and to whom.

{{ icon_chip('users', 'lavender') }}Adding another administrator

Administrators are never created through the public sign-up page. Someone with access to the server runs:

python create_admin.py new.admin@example.com "Full Name"
python create_admin.py existing@example.com --promote
