What Nephron does not do

Nephron estimates risk. It does not diagnose kidney disease, replace a physician, or make clinical decisions.

A High Preliminary Risk result is a reason to consider professional evaluation. A Low Preliminary Risk result does not mean you are free of kidney disease.

Start with a preliminary screening whenever you are ready.

It is a starting point, not a diagnosis - and you choose who, if anyone, sees the result.
